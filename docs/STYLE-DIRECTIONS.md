# Four tattoo style directions

The guest menu offers Fine Line, Old School, Traditional and Watercolor. The unchanged Catalog match default keeps eligible original catalog designs first before a user selects a direction. Legacy saved styles remain readable; they are not silently renamed or regenerated.

Fine Line asks for minimal delicate contours. Old School asks for classic American flash with bold outlines and limited flat fills. Traditional uses bold classic motifs and clean strong contours; eligible classic-flash catalog labels can overlap Old School, rather than inventing separate source provenance. Watercolor is a digital color-wash direction with visible source contours and soft painted washes. Its AI brief must allow washes/gradients rather than contradictory monochrome/no-shading rules.

The server checks applicable existing catalog records first. Only a valid no-match free-text request creates new AI artwork. Genuine new style creation depends on an available image service; this update does not establish that the earlier service restriction is resolved. Merely adding menus or validating prompts makes no provider call. Family/mandala exclusions and exact-ID mismatch protection remain in place.

A Watercolor result starts on its untouched painted source artwork. A separate selected-ink color version is still a flat local fill, not an AI watercolor texture, and the comparison button explicitly says Flat ink version. Staff may simplify a digital watercolor look for instant hand-drawing; no physical watercolor finish or drawing-time guarantee is made. Brighter custom inks, white background, preserved original lines, capability-protected source/color/artist files and original-derived mirrored printing remain unchanged.
